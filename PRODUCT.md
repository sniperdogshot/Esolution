# Esolution Engenharia

## Product purpose

One-page site that turns visitors into energy-solar quote requests (WhatsApp is the main channel).
Esolution is an engineering company in Eusébio/CE that designs and installs grid-tied photovoltaic
systems for homes and businesses in Fortaleza, Eusébio and the rest of Ceará.

## Users

Homeowners and small-business owners in Ceará, mostly on a phone, often outdoors in strong daylight,
who just got a high electricity bill (Enel Ceará) and want to know: how much would I save, is this
company serious, and how do I start. Not technical; they distrust exaggerated promises.

## Brand

- Voice: direct, sunny, engineered. Plain Portuguese, no jargon without explanation.
- Identity: the logo (white "solution" wordmark + teal-to-green stacked "E", `#00baa0` → `#30be5a`).
  The logo is white, so it always sits on dark green surfaces.
- Claims the client already uses: "economia de até 80%", "instalação segura e certificada",
  "retorno do investimento", "sem baterias". Do not invent numbers (installations, years, ratings).

## Anti-references

- Generic green-and-yellow solar templates with icon cards and stock smiles.
- Exaggerated "zere sua conta" promises.

## Strategic principles

1. Every section ends one step closer to the simulator or WhatsApp.
2. Answer the buyer's doubts (cloudy days, night, batteries, bill never zero) on the page.
3. Real project photos over decoration.
